Player Support

Need help with Gate Smash: Cursed Castle? Send a report through our GitHub support page.

When reporting a problem

Include your headset model, game version, what happened, and the steps that reproduce it. For leaderboard questions, mention the difficulty mode and the score concerned. Do not include passwords, payment information, or other sensitive details in a public issue.

Local data

Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game removes locally saved settings and progress. Online leaderboard entries are held by Meta and are separate from local data.
